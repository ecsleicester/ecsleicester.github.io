Summer 2026 — GCSE

Pupils at Emmanuel sit GCSEs with AQA and Eduqas at the end of Ash. The figures below cover the most recent examination series.

Read these numbers carefully.

Our GCSE cohorts are small, usually fewer than a dozen pupils. That means a single pupil can shift a percentage by ten points or more, and comparing our figures year on year, or against a school of a thousand pupils, tells you very little. We publish the number of pupils alongside every percentage so that you can see exactly what the figures rest on. We would rather you asked us about them than drew conclusions from them.

What the figures do not show

A grade tells you where a pupil finished. It does not tell you where they started, and for a number of our pupils that distance is the real achievement. Some arrive with us having found school difficult elsewhere. If you want to understand what we add rather than what we select, ask us about individual pupils when you visit. We will tell you honestly.
