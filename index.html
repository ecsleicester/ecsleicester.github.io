Who we are

A school small enough that no child is a stranger.

Emmanuel began as a prayer meeting in 2001. Two years later, in August 2003, the school opened its doors. It is still what those parents started: a school that belongs to families rather than to a chain, run by the Leicestershire Christian Education Trust.

Christ at the centre

Not an assembly bolted onto an ordinary school day. What we believe about God shapes how we teach history, science, art and everything else — and how we speak to a child having a difficult morning.

Small on purpose

Seven classes, and room for seventy-five children in the whole school. Every member of staff knows every child by name, and most will have taught them. Nobody drifts through unnoticed here.

Alongside parents

We hold that raising children is entrusted to parents, not to schools. Our job is to support that work with skill and care, not to take it over.

One school, seven classes

Twelve years, and we watch the whole thing.

A child can arrive at four and leave at sixteen. Our classes are mixed-age and named for trees, which suits us: growth is slow, it happens underground first, and it cannot be hurried.

What your child would learn

A full curriculum, taught by people who believe Christ holds it together.

Pupils here sit the same GCSEs as anywhere else and are taught the same subjects. What differs is the account we give of them. Our framework, Christ in All Things, takes its name from Colossians 1:17 — that in him all things hold together. In practice that means a science lesson is a science lesson, taught well, by someone who thinks the order they are studying was put there.

Admissions

Come and see the school on an ordinary day.

We would rather you saw us mid-week with the school running than at a polished open evening. Visits are with the Head Teacher and take about an hour.

1

Get in touch

Ring the school or send the enquiry form. Tell us your child’s age and anything we should know.

2

Look round

You will see classes teaching, meet staff, and have time to ask whatever you want to ask.

3

Apply

We give you the forms after the visit. Places are offered subject to the admissions policy.
