Why we have a uniform

Uniform settles the question of what to wear before it is asked, and it means nobody is judged in the playground on what their family can afford. We keep it simple and we keep it cheap.

Ordering

Branded items are ordered through the Parent Hub, using your family passcode. You choose the size and the personalisation, see a preview of how the printing will look, and pay by bank transfer using the reference the Hub generates for you.

Orders are placed in batches rather than one at a time, so there are ordering deadlines. add the link and the deadlines
