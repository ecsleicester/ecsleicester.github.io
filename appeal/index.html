Dear friends and supporters

This appeal was sent to the friends and supporters of Emmanuel Christian School in August 2026. It is reproduced here in full for those who would like to read it again or pass it on. The need it describes is ongoing, and the ways to respond at the end of the letter remain open.

We are writing to you with an important and urgent request for prayer and support for Emmanuel.

For many years Emmanuel has sought to provide a distinctly Christian education for children and young people in Leicester — a place where pupils are known and loved, where Christ is at the centre, and where children are encouraged to grow in faith as well as in learning.

We remain deeply committed to that calling, and we continue to believe passionately in the importance of Christian education.

But we believe it is right to be open with those who have faithfully prayed for and supported Emmanuel about the significant financial challenge the school is currently facing.

The pressures we face

Over the last two years the financial pressures on independent schools have increased considerably.

The introduction of VAT on independent school fees has increased the cost of education for our families and added further pressure to affordability and to pupil numbers. At the same time Emmanuel has faced substantial increases in employment costs, through higher employer National Insurance contributions and successive rises in the National Minimum and Living Wage.

These increased costs have come at a time when our pupil numbers are lower than we had hoped for this academic year. Our regular income has reduced while our essential costs have continued to rise.

For a small school, even a relatively small change in pupil numbers has a significant effect.

What we have already done

We want to reassure you that the Governors and Senior Leadership Team have not simply allowed these pressures to continue unchecked.

Over recent months we have undertaken a detailed review of expenditure and have already made a number of difficult decisions to reduce the school's costs. These have included a staff redundancy, reductions in staffing and salary costs, and savings across other areas of the school's operation.

These decisions have not been easy. Our staff have made sacrifices and have continued to serve the children of Emmanuel with remarkable commitment throughout a challenging period. We are extremely grateful for them.

Despite these measures, the financial position remains serious.

Where we stand

For a number of years Emmanuel has benefited from a substantial interest-free loan which has helped support the ongoing work of the school. That loan has now reached its agreed maximum of £120,000.

More recently, as cash-flow pressures have increased, the school has had to rely on additional short-term loans to meet essential commitments, including the payment of staff salaries. Those short-term loans now total approximately £29,000.

The school can no longer rely on increasing borrowing as the solution to its financial pressures.

We do not share these figures to create fear, nor to place pressure on anyone. But neither do we believe it would be right to hide the seriousness of the situation from those who love Emmanuel and have stood alongside the school over many years.

Without a significant improvement in our financial position, the future operation of Emmanuel Christian School could ultimately be at risk.

We are therefore asking our wider Emmanuel family to stand with us.

Please pray

Above everything else, we are asking you to pray. We believe Emmanuel belongs to God, and that throughout the history of the school He has faithfully provided.

Please pray specifically:

  • For God's provision for the immediate and longer-term financial needs of Emmanuel
  • For new families and pupils to join the school
  • For wisdom and discernment for the Governors as they make important decisions
  • For strength and encouragement for our staff
  • For our current families, as they continue to make significant sacrifices to provide Christian education for their children
  • That Emmanuel would continue to fulfil the purpose for which it was established

Prayer is not simply something we turn to when every other option has failed. We recognise our dependence on God, and we want our first response to this challenge to be to seek Him together.

Would you consider giving?

Alongside prayer, we would ask you to consider whether you may be able to support Emmanuel financially at this time.

For some this may mean a one-off gift. For others it may mean beginning to give regularly each month, or increasing an existing regular gift.

There is no expectation and no pressure. We simply want to make the need known, and allow people to respond as they feel led.

Even modest monthly gifts, multiplied across our wider community of supporters, could make a significant difference to the long-term sustainability of Emmanuel.

Give online

See the ways to give →

One-off gifts, regular giving and our bank details for standing orders are all there. If anything is unclear, ring the school on 0116 222 0792.

If you are a UK taxpayer we can claim Gift Aid on eligible donations, adding 25p to every pound at no cost to you.

One word of caution. If you ever receive an email that appears to come from the school giving different account details, or asking you to change where you send a standing order, please ring us before sending anything. We will never ask you to change our bank details by email.

Any gifts received will support the ongoing charitable work and financial stability of Emmanuel Christian School, and will be used by the charity where they are most needed.

Why Emmanuel still matters

Although this letter necessarily speaks about finances, we do not want finances to become the focus of what Emmanuel is about.

Every number represents children.

Every day, pupils come through the doors of Emmanuel and are taught that they are created in the image of God. They learn within a community where teachers know them personally, where relationships matter, where God's Word is valued, and where we seek to help each child become the person God has created them to be.

We see children growing in confidence. We see them developing academically. We see young people asking questions about faith and discovering more about Christ. We see families being supported. And we see opportunities to reach children and families across Leicester with the hope of the Gospel.

That is why we believe Emmanuel is worth fighting for.

Our vision remains: Growing in Christ. Thriving in Learning. Shaping the Future.

We continue to believe that God has a purpose for Emmanuel, and that there is more still to come. But we have reached a point where we need to share the reality of our situation with those who care about the school.

Will you stand with us?

We do not know exactly how God will provide for the need before us. But throughout Emmanuel's history there have been moments when the way forward seemed uncertain, and time and again God has faithfully provided through His people.

So we are asking you: will you pray for Emmanuel? Will you consider whether you are able to give? Will you share our need with others who believe in the importance of Christian education? And will you stand with us as we seek God for the future of the school?

Thank you for every prayer that has been prayed, every gift that has been given, every word of encouragement, and every way in which you have supported Emmanuel over the years. We are deeply grateful.

Please continue to pray with us that God would make a way forward, and that Emmanuel Christian School would continue for many years to come — serving children, supporting Christian families, and making Christ known.

With our love and gratitude,

Andy Harris Headteacher

On behalf of the Governors of Emmanuel Christian School

August 2026
