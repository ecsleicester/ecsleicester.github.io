GCSE courses

Birch is Year 9, Ash is Year 10 and Cedar is Year 11. Pupils work towards GCSEs examined with AQA and Eduqas. Our option list is shorter than a large secondary’s. That is a real consequence of being small, and we would rather say so than let you discover it in Year 9.

Small groups

A GCSE set here might be six or eight pupils. Nobody sits quietly at the back, and a misunderstanding does not survive a fortnight unnoticed. It also means a pupil cannot coast, which some find harder than they expected.

Examinations and access arrangements

Our Examinations Officer is Libby Harris. Pupils who qualify for access arrangements — extra time, a reader, a separate room — are assessed for them in good time rather than at the last minute.

After Year 11

We do not have a sixth form. Pupils move on to sixth form colleges, further education colleges, apprenticeships or employment with training, and receive impartial careers guidance covering all of those routes.
