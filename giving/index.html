Giving to Emmanuel

The school was started by families who gave what they had. It has been carried by people who believe in it ever since.

Why it matters

Fees cover the ordinary running of the school, and they are deliberately kept low — a fraction of what most independent schools charge, so that Christian education is possible for families who could not otherwise consider it.

That choice has a consequence. It means the school has always depended on people who give.

Gifts pay for the things fees cannot stretch to: the outside space the children play on, resources in classrooms, and the margin that lets a small school keep going through a difficult term. Every pupil here benefits from somebody's generosity, whether they know it or not.

Ways to give

One-off gifts and regular giving, whichever suits you.

Regular giving, even in small amounts, is the most useful of all. A school can plan around it in a way it cannot plan around one-off gifts, however generous.

Giving by bank transfer

If you would rather give directly by bank transfer, or set up a standing order, our account details are on the giving page along with the other ways to give.

All the ways to give

See the ways to give →

If anything is unclear, or you would rather have our details directly from us, email office@ecsleicester.org.uk or ring the school on 0116 222 0792.

One word of caution. If you ever receive an email that appears to come from the school giving different account details, or asking you to change where you send a standing order, please ring us before sending anything. We will never ask you to change our bank details by email.

Gift Aid

If you are a UK taxpayer, we can claim Gift Aid on eligible donations. That adds 25p to every pound you give, at no cost to you.

Ask us for a Gift Aid declaration form, or tick the option where the giving platform offers it.

Legacies and larger gifts

If you are considering remembering the school in your will, or making a larger gift towards a particular project, please speak to the Head Teacher. These conversations are always private and never pressured.

The school's current projects, including the outdoor space and the planned annexe, are described on the Support us page.

Praying for the school

Emmanuel began as a prayer meeting in 2001, two years before it had a building or a pupil, and it has never stopped depending on that.

Our termly prayer newsletter carries specific things to pray for. It goes to churches, former parents, grandparents and anyone else who asks.
