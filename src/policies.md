## Our policies {#about}

<p class="lead">Everything we are required to publish, and a few things we are not but think you should be able to read anyway. All documents are PDFs. Each shows when it was last reviewed and when it is next due.</p>

<div class="note">
<p><b>Before publishing this page</b></p>
<p>Convert every policy from Word to PDF, upload it, and enter the review dates. A Word file on a school website can be edited after download and looks unfinished. Review dates that have passed are worse than no dates at all, so check each one before it goes up.</p>
</div>
