## Safeguarding and welfare {#safeguarding}

Safeguarding is the first responsibility of any school. If you have a concern about a child, contact Andy Harris, Designated Safeguarding Lead, on 0116 222 0792. If a child is at immediate risk, telephone the police on 999.

## Something not here? {#ask}

We also hold a number of policies that relate to the employment of staff rather than to pupils or families &mdash; absence management, disciplinary and grievance procedures, and others. These are available on request.

If there is a policy you would like to see and it is not on this page, ask us. There is nothing we hold back from parents as a matter of course.
