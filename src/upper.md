## GCSE courses {#gcse}

Option subjects run subject to numbers. Design and technology, art and Biblical Studies are taught throughout the school but are not currently offered at GCSE.

Option subjects run subject to numbers. Our cohorts are small, and we would rather be straight about that than list a subject we cannot staff in a given year.

## Small groups {#teaching}

A GCSE set here might be six or eight pupils. Nobody sits quietly at the back, and a misunderstanding does not survive a fortnight unnoticed. It also means a pupil cannot coast, which some find harder than they expected.

## Choosing options {#choosing}

Options are chosen during Year 8, in Maple. Pupils and parents are given an options letter, and we talk it through with each family. We also ask families to pray about it together before deciding.

## Examinations and access arrangements {#exams}

Our Examinations Officer is Libby Harris. Pupils who qualify for access arrangements — extra time, a reader, a separate room — are assessed for them in good time rather than at the last minute.

## After Year 11 {#after}

We do not have a sixth form. Pupils move on to sixth form colleges, further education colleges, apprenticeships or employment with training, and receive impartial careers guidance covering all of those routes.
