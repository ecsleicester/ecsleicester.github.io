## Our statement of faith {#statement}

<p class="lead">Emmanuel Christian School is an evangelical Christian school. Every member of staff subscribes to the statement of faith of the Leicestershire Christian Education Trust, and it shapes what is taught and how the school is run.</p>

## What this means day to day {#practice}

A statement of faith on a website tells you very little. What it looks like in a school is more useful.

- The Bible is taught as true, and taught properly, with attention to what it actually says.
- We hold to the biblical account of creation, and pupils are taught it. They are also taught the scientific theories they will meet at GCSE and beyond, accurately and in full, because a pupil who cannot give a fair account of a view they do not hold has not been educated in it.
- Worship happens daily. It is not performance, and children are not pressured into professions of faith.
- Christian conviction shows up most in how a child is treated when they have got something badly wrong.

## Pupils and families of other faiths, and of none {#others}

We admit children from families who do not share our beliefs, and they are treated no differently once they are here. Nobody is required to profess faith, and nobody is thought less of for not having it.

From Year 7 pupils study the major world religions. We teach them as their adherents would recognise them, not as caricatures to be knocked down. Pupils should be able to explain what a Muslim, a Hindu or an atheist believes and why they find it convincing. Understanding a view properly is a precondition of disagreeing with it honestly.

## Faith and life in modern Britain {#values}

We actively promote the values of democracy, the rule of law, individual liberty, and mutual respect and tolerance of those with different faiths and beliefs. This is not a box we tick alongside our Christian convictions; we hold that respecting people who differ from us follows directly from believing every person is made in God’s image.

Pupils here will meet views that conflict with what they are taught at home and at school. We would rather they met them in a classroom, explained fairly by a teacher who knows them, than for the first time at eighteen.

## Staff and the statement {#staff}

Teaching and support staff are appointed on the basis of their agreement with the statement of faith as well as their professional competence. Where a role has an occupational requirement of this kind, it is stated in the advertisement.
