## What the trustees do {#does}

- Set the direction of the school and hold the Head Teacher to account for it.
- Approve the budget and oversee the school’s finances.
- Ensure the school meets the Independent School Standards.
- Oversee safeguarding, including an annual review of policy and practice.
- Appoint the Head Teacher and approve senior appointments.

Trustees are unpaid. They visit the school during the working day, meet staff, and see the school running rather than only reading about it.

## Safeguarding governance {#safeguarding}

Caroline Vickers is the trustee with specific responsibility for safeguarding. She meets the Designated Safeguarding Lead termly, reviews the safeguarding record, and reports to the full trustee body.

Safeguarding concerns about a member of staff, including the Head Teacher, should be raised with the Chair of Trustees.

## School development {#plan}

The trustees agree the School Development Plan and review progress against it through the year. The current plan runs from 2026 to 2029 and follows the three parts of our vision, with priorities covering regulatory compliance and safeguarding, quality of education, and Christian distinctiveness.
