## Parent Hub {#hub}

<p class="lead">Kit orders, absences and leave requests all go through the Parent Hub. One passcode covers your whole family, so if you have more than one child here you only sign in once.</p>

<div class="note">
<p><b>Open the Parent Hub</b></p>
<p style="margin:0"><a class="btn btn-gold" href="https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfycbyFia18Og2msKpA2PTSui3ltNBhh9Cn1G652KY4EbvmWkS4EnAaeXTRzV7mShe6DgxCyg/exec" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the Parent Hub &rarr;</a></p>
<p style="margin-top:14px">Add it to your phone’s home screen and it behaves like an app. On an iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.</p>

## Talking to us {#talk}

The quickest route is almost always your child’s class teacher or form tutor. For anything urgent, ring the school — do not rely on email being seen the same day.

- Telephone: [0116 222 0792](tel:01162220792)
- Email: [office@ecsleicester.org.uk](mailto:office@ecsleicester.org.uk)
- Something urgent about safety: ask for Andy Harris, Designated Safeguarding Lead.

If we are worried about your child, you will hear it from us early and in person. We would ask the same of you.

## Reports and consultations {#reports}

You receive a written report each year and are invited to consultation evenings each term. From September 2026 you can also see your child’s assessment information as it is recorded, through Go4Schools, rather than waiting for the report.

<div class="note">
<p><b>Go4Schools</b></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:14px"><a class="btn btn-navy" href="https://www.go4schools.com/parents/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Go4Schools parent login →</a></p>
<p style="margin:0"><b>Signing in for the first time:</b> click <em>First-time user</em>, enter the email address the school holds for you, and you will be sent a link to create a password. After that you sign in with your email address and that password.</p>
<p style="margin-top:12px;margin-bottom:0">If the email does not arrive, check your junk folder. If it still does not, ring the school — it usually means we hold a different address for you.</p>
</div>
