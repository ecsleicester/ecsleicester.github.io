## Requesting leave {#leave}

Leave in term time — a medical appointment, a family occasion, anything planned — is requested through the Parent Hub rather than by letter. The request goes to the Head Teacher and you are emailed once a decision has been made.

## When to keep your child at home {#whenkeep}

The general rule is simple: if your child is well enough to take part in the school day, send them. Children who are miserable but not infectious usually do better in school than at home.

The exclusion periods below follow national guidance on infections in schools. <span class="todo">Check against the current UK Health Security Agency guidance before publishing</span>

<tbody>
<tr><td class="num">Vomiting or diarrhoea</td><td>48 hours after the last episode</td></tr>
<tr><td class="num">Chickenpox</td><td>Until all the spots have crusted over, usually about five days after they appear</td></tr>
<tr><td class="num">High temperature</td><td>Until it has settled and they feel well enough</td></tr>
<tr><td class="num">Impetigo</td><td>Until lesions are crusted or 48 hours after starting treatment</td></tr>
<tr><td class="num">Conjunctivitis</td><td>No need to stay at home</td></tr>
<tr><td class="num">Head lice</td><td>No need to stay at home. Please treat and tell us.</td></tr>
<tr><td class="num">Threadworms</td><td>No need to stay at home once treatment has started</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

If you are unsure, ring us. We would rather have the conversation than have you guess.

## Medication in school {#medication}

We can administer prescribed medication where a form has been completed and the medicine is in its original labelled packaging. Nothing is given without written consent.

## First aid {#firstaid}

Trained first aiders are on site throughout the school day. If your child is hurt beyond a minor scrape you will be told the same day, not at the end of the week.

Our trained first aiders are Sarah Seeds, George Sutton, Mia Seeds and Ruth Allen.

## Allergies {#allergies}

Tell us in writing about any allergy, however minor it seems, and update us when things change. <span class="todo">Confirm the school’s position on nuts and other allergens</span>

## Lateness {#lateness}

Registration closes at 9.00am. Children arriving after that should come to the office so that we know they are in the building.

## Attendance {#attendance}

Attendance is not a statistic we chase for its own sake. In a small mixed-age class, a child who misses a fortnight is genuinely hard to catch up, and we would rather work with you early than write letters later. If your child is finding it hard to come in, tell us. That is a problem we can help with.
