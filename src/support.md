## Pray {#pray}

<p class="lead">The school began as a prayer meeting in 2001, two years before it had a building or a pupil. It has never stopped depending on that.</p>

Our termly prayer newsletter carries specific things to pray for — staff, pupils, finances, decisions in front of the trustees. It goes to churches, former parents, grandparents and anyone else who asks.

## The building extension {#extension}

God has been good to us over the years, and the foundations for a two-storey annexe are already in the ground. We are waiting on the Lord for the right time to build on them.

In the meantime those foundations are doing useful work: our portakabin classrooms sit on them, which is what has allowed the school to grow while we wait. Building the annexe itself would cost in the region of £700,000.

### Our outside space

Closer to hand, and considerably more achievable, is the ground the children play on every day.

In October 2025 we laid an area of tarmac, and the difference it has made is hard to overstate. We would like to go further, in two stages.

## Vacancies {#vacancies}

There are no vacancies at present. When posts come up they are advertised here, with a closing date.

Emmanuel Christian School is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children, and expects all staff and volunteers to share that commitment. All appointments are subject to safer recruitment checks, including an enhanced DBS check with children’s barred list information, and satisfactory references.

Teaching and support posts carry an occupational requirement relating to our statement of faith, which is stated in each advertisement. [Read the statement of faith →](/faith/)

## Helping in school {#volunteer}

We always welcome volunteers, whether that is supporting teaching staff in class or helping with the many jobs that keep a school running.

There is a form to complete and a DBS check to be done before you can start. That is not a formality — every adult who spends time around children here goes through it, and we would not want it otherwise.

If you have time to give, ring the school or speak to the Head Teacher.
