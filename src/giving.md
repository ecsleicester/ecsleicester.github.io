## Why it matters {#why}

<p class="lead">Fees cover the ordinary running of the school, and they are deliberately kept low — a fraction of what most independent schools charge, so that Christian education is possible for families who could not otherwise consider it.</p>

That choice has a consequence. It means the school has always depended on people who give.

Gifts pay for the things fees cannot stretch to: the outside space the children play on, resources in classrooms, and the margin that lets a small school keep going through a difficult term. Every pupil here benefits from somebody's generosity, whether they know it or not.

## Giving by bank transfer {#bank}

If you would rather give directly by bank transfer or set up a standing order, please ask us and we will send you our account details.

<div class="note">
<p><b>Request our bank details</b></p>
<p style="margin-bottom:0">Email <a class="inline" href="mailto:office@ecsleicester.org.uk?subject=Bank%20details%20for%20giving">office@ecsleicester.org.uk</a> or ring the school on <a class="inline" href="tel:01162220792">0116 222 0792</a>.</p>
</div>

**Why we do not publish them.** Bank details on a public web page are a well-known route for fraud: a page is copied, an account number is altered by a digit or two, and the gift goes to somebody else entirely. Charities and churches lose money to this every year, and it is the giver who suffers.

Asking us directly takes a moment and means you have our details from us. If you ever receive an email that appears to come from the school with different account details, please ring us before sending anything.

## Gift Aid {#giftaid}

If you are a UK taxpayer, we can claim Gift Aid on eligible donations. That adds 25p to every pound you give, at no cost to you.

Ask us for a Gift Aid declaration form, or tick the option where the giving platform offers it.

## Legacies and larger gifts {#legacy}

If you are considering remembering the school in your will, or making a larger gift towards a particular project, please speak to the Head Teacher. These conversations are always private and never pressured.

The school's current projects, including the outdoor space and the planned annexe, are described on the [Support us](/support/) page.

## Praying for the school {#pray}

Emmanuel began as a prayer meeting in 2001, two years before it had a building or a pupil, and it has never stopped depending on that.

Our termly prayer newsletter carries specific things to pray for. It goes to churches, former parents, grandparents and anyone else who asks.
