## Who inspects us {#who}

As an independent school we are inspected by the Independent Schools Inspectorate, which reports to the Department for Education. ISI inspects against the Independent School Standards and publishes its findings.

Reports are also available directly from the ISI website, and we would encourage you to look there as well as here.

## Reading an inspection report {#reading}

Inspection reports are written for the Department for Education rather than for parents, and they are worth reading with that in mind. They tell you whether a school meets the standards it is required to meet. They tell you rather less about what a school is like to be a child in.

So read the report, then come and visit. If there is something in a report you want to ask about, ask. We would far rather answer it than have you wonder.
