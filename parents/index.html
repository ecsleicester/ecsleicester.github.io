Parent Hub

Kit orders, absences and leave requests all go through the Parent Hub. One passcode covers your whole family, so if you have more than one child here you only sign in once.

Open the Parent Hub

Insert the Parent Hub link

Add it to your phone’s home screen and it behaves like an app. On an iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

What you can do in the Hub
  
Order kitPE t-shirts and hoodies for each of your children, with names or initials added. The price updates as you choose, and you see how the printing will look before you order.
Report an absenceTell us when your child is unwell or has an appointment. It reaches the office and senior staff straight away, and you get a confirmation email.
Request leaveAsk for leave in term time. The request goes to the Head Teacher and you are emailed once a decision is made.
Your detailsChange your name or email address. If an email address changes, the old one is notified.

Passcodes are issued to each family by the school. If yours is not working, use the forgotten passcode link and it will be sent to your registered address — or ring the school rather than struggling with it.

Talking to us

The quickest route is almost always your child’s class teacher or form tutor. For anything urgent, ring the school — do not rely on email being seen the same day.

  • Telephone: 0116 222 0792
  • Email: office@ecsleicester.org.uk
  • Something urgent about safety: ask for Andy Harris, Designated Safeguarding Lead.

If we are worried about your child, you will hear it from us early and in person. We would ask the same of you.

Reports and consultations

You receive a written report each year and are invited to consultation evenings each term. From September 2026 you can also see your child’s assessment information as it is recorded, through Go4Schools, rather than waiting for the report.
